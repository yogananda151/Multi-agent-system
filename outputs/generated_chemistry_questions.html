In the first-order reaction shown, what percentage of reactant A has reacted after 60 min?
(A) 75%
(B) 93.75%
(C) 87.5%
(D) 50%
Correct Answer: (C)

Question 2 • [IMAGE]
The graph shows a first-order reaction ln([A]/[A]₀) vs time. The rate constant k is
(A) 0.0200 min⁻¹
(B) 0.0400 min⁻¹
(C) 0.0600 min⁻¹
(D) 0.0800 min⁻¹
Correct Answer: (B)
Explanation: For first-order, slope = -k. Here slope = -2.40/60 = -0.0400, so k = 0.0400 min⁻¹.
