From the given Arrhenius plot (ln k vs 1/T), the activation energy Ea is closest to
(A) 80 kJ mol⁻¹
(B) 100 kJ mol⁻¹
(C) 120 kJ mol⁻¹
(D) 200 kJ mol⁻¹
Correct Answer: (B)
Explanation: Slope = -Ea/R = -12027.9. Ea = -slope × 8.314 J/mol = 100 kJ/mol.
Question 2 • [IMAGE]
The graph shows a first-order reaction ln([A]/[A]₀) vs time. The rate constant k is
(A) 0.0150 min⁻¹
(B) 0.0300 min⁻¹
(C) 0.0450 min⁻¹
(D) 0.0600 min⁻¹
Correct Answer: (B)
Explanation: For first-order, slope = -k. Here slope = -1.80/60 = -0.0300, so k = 0.0300 min⁻¹.
